What does Work Mode record?

Records, only while Work Mode is on:

  • The application in front, and how long you're on it
  • Window title and file/document name, when the OS allows it
  • The domain (not the full page) of the active browser tab, for supported browsers
  • Whether your keyboard/mouse have been idle — never what you typed or clicked
  • Your current InternOps task/project

Never records, ever:

  • Passwords, keystrokes, or clipboard content
  • Screenshots or screen recordings
  • Microphone or camera
  • Anything at all before Start or after End Shift

The window-title and browser-domain signals need extra OS permission (Accessibility / per-app Automation) and simply won't appear if you haven't granted it — the shift itself still tracks fine either way.
